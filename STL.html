A personal message to STL students:

Welcome to STL College Helper. I wanted to write this to you guys so you can have a more specific idea of how to look at your future. I know that there are a lot of you who have no clue what you want to do after high school, and I knew that that is a big issue when everyone is asking you about it. So, I wanted to ake this websiite to help you find your way, your path, your journey. Even if what happens isn't what you planned, I wanted you guys to be prepared for what comes after we walk across the field. So, I hope this website can be a resource for you to find your way, and I hope it can be a place where you can find the information you need to make informed decisions about your future. With all of that in mind, these are the stats of our specific high school, so when we say "This college wants a lot of AP courses" or "This college wants a lot of clubs", you won't stress because our high school doesn't offer exactly what a college wants. Remember that colleges get your transcript, and they know what STL offers. So, colleges don't just get test scores and grades. They get the entire context of you as a student. So, STL offers(as of June 2026):

  • 23 AP/IB Courses
  • Over 50 clubs and activities
  • 8 Career Pathways
  • 28 Sports
  • And a lot more

So, why am I telling you this when you already know it? Because when you hear on TikTok that you need 39 AP courses and active leadership in 15 clubs, and you begin stressing because you feel you won't make it, you can look here to see what STL has. No college will punish you because you didn't take a class that wasn't offered at your school.
