Columbia University

Columbia University is a private Ivy League research university in New York City. It is one of the most prestigious universities in the world and has a strong reputation for academic excellence.

Applicant Information

Similar to all of the other Ivy League universities, Columbia is highly selective and has an acceptance rate of 4%.

Columbia considers the following factors in their admissions process:

  • GPA: Average of 3.95
  • SAT/ACT: Average of 1530 SAT and 34 ACT
  • Academic Rigor: Columbia expects a rigorous courseload throughout all four years of high school.
  • Personal Traits: Columbia is focused on admitting students who fit their campus community and will contribute positively to it.
  • Holistic Review: Columbia employs a holistic review process to evaluate each applicant with context and shows what they've done in their environment.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

Columbia, like many other universities, claims to be need blind, however they do ask if you need financial assistance on their application. Families making under $150,000 per year usually pay no tuition, and the average net price is close to $20,000 per year. Columbia also offers merit scholarships, which are awarded based on academic achievement and other factors.

Overall, Columbia is a highly prestigious institution and is a reach school for all students, but its prominent fame and academic reputation make it a highly attractive choice.
