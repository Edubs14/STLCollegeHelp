Jesus Loves All

Look around you. There are the most beautiful wonders of nature and science around you. The fact that I can publish this and have someone in China see it is a marvel on its own. To think that all this is here, and a loving God decided it needed one of you too. There are times where you will be challeneged. You will be challenged in many ways, and it is up to you to decide how to respond. You can choose to run to Him, and He will not magically take the problem away, but He will guide you through it. Or you can choose to go it on your own. He will not get angry at you. He will not betray you. He will not leave you. He will stand there, with open arms, waiting for you. And get this. He's not the only one who can help you. He gave you people to gp to, family, friends, trusted adults, so they can give you the comfort you need. He paid the price for your sins, and He did it out of pure love. If you're wating on something, or someone, in your life, He will give it to you, if you're ready. I've asked Him to help me and to igve me people or things in my life so many times. Sure, I'm still waiting on a few things, but I know that He will give them to me when I'm ready. And I know that He will give them to you too, when you're ready.

For those who do not know His love, I cannot emphasize enough how much He loves you, and how much being with Him will change every aspect of your life. But you have to commit to Him. It's like having a wife/husband. It's a sacred relationship, and you have to be committed to Him, obeying Him. If you fall, He will help you back up, if you ask Him. He will never refuse to forgive you. But you have to give Him your heart, and it is not fun. But to be in paradise forever, it's a small ask.

For those struggling with their faith, I know how hard it is. I know how hard it is to believe in something you cannot see. But I can tell you that He is real, and He loves you. And if you ask Him to help you, He will. You just have to be willing to listen to Him, and obey Him. Try your best to stay focused on Him. Nobody else loves you like He does. So keep strong, my brother/sister, and know that He will always be there for you.

For those who are struggling with sin, I know that struggle personally. Sin is a hole you keep falling into, and each time you do you make it a little deeper. But He is a ladder, and He will always be there to bail you out. The key is to counicate with Him, keep praying. The most overlooked part of prayer is the vast number of Saints. They all correlate to a value, virtue, or a struggle. While it seems strange, a quick google seach will show you who has fought your fight before. So pray to Him, and to them. I belive in all of you.
