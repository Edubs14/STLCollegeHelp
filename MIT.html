Massachusetts Institute of Technology

MIT is a private research university located in Cambridge, Massachusetts. It is known for its world renowned advancements in science and technology, as well as a high level of academic excellence.

Applicant Information

MIT is an extremely competitive school, with standards similar to the Ivy League and other top-tier institutions. It has an acceptance rate of around 4.5%.

MIT considers many factors, but the key ones are:

  • GPA: A top 10 percent of your graduating class is average, or a 4.17.
  • SAT/ACT Scores: High scores are expected. A 1540 on the SAT or a 35 on the ACT is average.
  • Core Values: MIT looks at your essays and personal statements to see if you are collaborative, failure resilient, and creative. However, they do emphasize that you do not pretend to be someone you are not when writing your application.
  • Extracurriculars: MIT, similar to other top-tier institutions, looks for students who are passionate about their extracurriculars and have a strong commitment to them, usually showing in leadership roles or passion projects.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

MIT offers no merit, athletic, or other scholarships. However, they are unique in that they are completely need-blind, meaning they do not consider your ability to pay, and provide comprehensive financial aid to all students who demonstrate a need.

Families making less than $100,00 a year pay nothing for both tuition and room and board, and families making between $100,000 and $200,000 pay only additional costs, with full tuition covered.*

*On average.

Overall, MIT is an extremely difficult school to be accepted into and is a reach school for every student. MIT offers top-tier education in science and technology, but is extremly selective.
