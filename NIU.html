Northern Illinois University

Northern Illinois University (NIU) is a public research university located in DeKalb, Illinois. Founded in 1895, it has since grown to become one of the largest universities in Illinois. NIU offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs across various disciplines, including business, engineering, education, health sciences, and more. The university is known for its focus on research and its programs connecting students to professors for resarch opportunities.

Applicant Information

NIU is not very competetive, and it is not very diificult to be accepted They have an acceptance rate of 69.8%. With an average GPA between 2.98 to 3.33, and an automatic acceptance for students with a 3.0 GPA or higher, excluding those who apply for their limited nursing program. If a student has a GPA below 3.0, they are still considered through a holistic review process.

NIU has a test-optional policy, meaning that students are not required to submit standardized test scores (such as the SAT or ACT) as part of their application. However, if students choose to submit their test scores, they will be considered as part of the application review process.

Cost and Financial Aid

NIU offers various financial aid options to help students manage the cost of attendance. These include both athletic and merit-based scholarships. The university is committed to making higher education accessible and affordable for all students.

In addition to scholarships, NIU provides need-based financial aid, which is determined by the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).

NIU offers a large scholarship range based on GPA. With a 4.0, students are given $8,000 per year. The lowest scholarship amount is $1,000 per year if a student has a GPA between 3.0-3.24.

NIU also gives athletic scholarships to qualified student-athletes.

Overall, NIU is a good option for a safety school for highly qualified students, and a great option for those with a lower GPA who are looking for a more acessible and affordable school.
