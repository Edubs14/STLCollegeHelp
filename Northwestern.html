Northwestern University

Northwestern University is a private research university located in Evanston, Illinois. It was founded in 1851 and is known for its strong programs in journalism, engineering, and the performing arts. The university has a diverse student body and offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs.

Applicant Information

Northwestern University is extremely competitive, with an application critiqued in a similar way to Ivy League schools. They have an accptance rate of 7.7%.

Northwestern considers the following:

  • SAT/ACT Scores: The average SAT score is a 1530, and the average ACT score is a 34. Northwestern does automatically superscore the SAT, but not the ACT, unless the ACT is superscored offically.Their policy is different, so please look at their official website: Northwestern Application Requirements
  • Letters of Recommendation: 1 letter from your counselor, and 1 from a teacher in your chosen field.
  • "Fit": Northwestern looks for students who will contribute to their campus community and align with their values. They look for academic growth, ivolvement, and personal qualitites.
  • Early Decision: Northwestern trends to admit more students through their early decision process.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

Most students recieve the majority of their discount through financial aid or outside scholarships, as there are limited institutional scholarships available. Most are only offered to students from specific Chicago public schools. The average total scholarhsip, grant, and other monetary aid recieved for students' whose parents make between $90,000 and $120,000 is $57,440.

Overall, Northwestern University is a reach school for many students, but it is a highly respected institution with strong programs in various fields. It is worth applying, as they offer excellent opportunities at a decent price.
