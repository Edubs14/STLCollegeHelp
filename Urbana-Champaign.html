University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

The University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) is a public research university located in Champaign, Illinois. It is the flagship institution of the University of Illinois system and is known for its strong programs in engineering, computer science, business, and agriculture.

Applicant information

UIUC has a competitive admissions process, and it is fairly difficult to be accepted. They have an acceptance rate of 42.4%.

UIUC considers the following:

  • Course Rigor: Taking harder classes shows commitment to a future career and acedemic interest.
  • Extracurriculars: Showing real involvement in activites outside of class that relate to your major shows planning skills.
  • Standardized Test Scores: UIUC accepts either the SAT or ACT, and they consider these scores when deciding financial aid and/or scholarships.
  • GPA: Your GPA should be within their middle 50%, 3.7-4.0, to be a fair fit for UIUC.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

UIUC offers a range of scholarships, and you are automatically considered for them based on your GPA and your high school achievements. They range in value from a full tuition to several thousand dollars. Some require students to be Illinois residents, others require completeing the FAFSA, and some require demonstating a financial need.

Overall, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign is a great option for a match school for most and a safety school for the advanced.
