Yale University

Yale University is a private Ivy League research university in New Haven, Connecticut, founded in 1701. It is known worldwide as another top-tier institution. While it has excellent STEM programs, its historical reputation centers heavily on writing, government, and the arts.

Applicant information

Yale University is extremely competitve and is similar to Harvard in its diffivult and critical admissions process with an acceptance rate of 3.9%

Yale University considers the following:

  • GPA: While Yale does not publicly state their students' GPA's, most students graduate in the top 10% of their class.
  • SAT/ACT Scores: To be accepted, be within the middle 50% of admitted students's scores, which is typically between 1540-1560 on the SAT and 34-35 on the ACT.
  • Transcript: A high-quality transcript is highly reccomended, with both a high GPA and strong courses.
  • Extracurriculars: Yale looks for students who are passionate about their extracurriculars and have a strong commitment to them, usually showing in leadership roles or passion projects.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

Yale offers scholarships and grants, mostly need-based. They, like many other universities, cover 100% of the tuition that they believe you cannot pay. They factor many things into this aid, and they also offer both term-time jobs as well as education loans. If you recieve outside scholarships, they may or may not be deducted from your financial aid package.

Overall, similar to Harvard, Yale is a reach school for almost every student. It is a highly respected school globally, and is a top choice besides the tough journey it is to be accepted.
