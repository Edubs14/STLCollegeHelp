Harvard University

Harvard University is one of the most prestigious universities in the world. LOcated in Cambridge, Massachusetts and founded in 1636, it is renowned globally for its prestigious academic programs, offering undergraduate, graduate, and professional degrees across numerous fields.

Applicant information

Harvard University is extremely competitive with an acceptance rate of 4.2%, with an average GPA of 4.2 or higher. Harvard University considers students' academic achievement, intellectual curiosity, extracurricular involvement, leadership, and personal character.

Harvard is an extremely competitive school and is only reachable for the best and most devoted students Its acceptance rate is 3.6%.

Harvard University considers the following:

  • Course rigor: taking between 7-12 AP/IB courses over four years of high school is highly recommended.
  • Extracurriculars: Showing exceptional achievement or a leadership role in 1 or 2 activities that reflect your strengths and career interests.
  • Standardized Test Scores: Having a SAT score of:1500-1600 or ACT score of 34-36, is required for consideration for acceptance, and they look at these scores when deciding financial aid and/or scholarships.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

Harvard offers a range of scholarships, and you are automatically considered for need-based aid that aims to cover 100% of the tuition that you cannot pay. Harvard factors parent contribution (how much your parents make), student contribution (how much you can make), and other unusual circumstances. This adds up to Harvard covering what they believe you cannot afford, and leving you with a managable tuition. If you recieve any outside awards, they will be considered when you apply for financial aid, and they may reduce the amount of aid you recieve from Harvard.

Overall, Harvard is an extremly diificult school to get into and is a reach school for almost every stident. However, its presigious acedemics and worldwide fame make it an extremely appealing school.
