Princeton University

Princeton University is a private Ivy Leauge reserach university located in Princeton, New Jersey, founded in 1746. It is renowned for its destinguished faculty and alumni, including numerous Nobel Laureates, U.S. presidents, and influential figures across academia, politics, and business.

Applicant information

Princeton University is extremely competitive school with an acceptance rate of 4.6-5%

Princeton considers the following factors for admission:

  • GPA: Unweighted GPA of 3.93+, and a transcript featuring multiple AP or IB courses.
  • SAT/ACT: Getting high SAT scores of 1505+ or ACT scores of 34+ is average.
  • Core values: Princeton looks at two main essay prompts(one acadmeic interest essay and one about your experiences shaping your personality), plus three short-answer questons.
  • Extracurriculars: Princeton is seeking students that show strong interest and depth in a small number of clubs and activities.They want to see that you've made intense commitment to yourself and your community.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

Princeton offers no merit, or other scholarships. However, they are unique in that they completely offered financial aid based on a student's family income.

Families making under $150,000 pay no tutition, and those whose parents make more than that still tend to pay very little in tutition.

Overall, Princeton, just like all other Ivy League Schools, is a great school with stellar acedemics. However, it is a reach school, but is affordable for many.
