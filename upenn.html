University of Pennsylvania

The University of Pennsylvania is one of the America's most prestigious universities located in Philadelphia.

It is renowned for its strongest in business & Finance, Medicine & Health Sciences, Law, Biomedical & Life sciences, Engineering, Social Sciences & Psychology, Communication, and History.

Applicant information

University of Pennsylvania is extremely competitve school with an acceptance rate of 5%.

Here are the factors that they consider:

  • GPA: 3.93 is average.
  • SAt/ACT: An SAT score of 1550+ or ACT of 34 is average.
  • Extracurriculars & Activities: Leadership roles in club or community organizations, demonstrated passion, research experience, and awards all make your application stand out.
  • Essays: Universty of Pennsylvania requires three supplemental essays: Thank-You Note, Community Essay, and Why Penn/Why Major.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

Univeristy of Pennsylvania does not offer merit scholarships. However, they offer financial aid based on family income.

UPenn offers a complete, no expenses at all, aid to all families making under $75,000, and a package covering all tutition and sometimes more for all families making under $200,000.

Overall, University of Pennsylvania is a great reach school for every student. It offers great acedemics and a generous financial aid program.
