Ivy Leagues

The Ivy League is a group of eight prestigious universities in the United States. They are known for their rigorous academics, selective admissions, and strong alumni networks.

Harvard University

Located in Cambridge, Massachusetts, Harvard is the oldest institution of higher education in the United States.

Yale University

Located in New Haven, Connecticut, Yale is known for its strong programs in the arts and humanities.

Princeton University

Located in Princeton, New Jersey, Princeton is renowned for its focus on undergraduate education.

Columbia University

Located in New York City, Columbia is known for its strong journalism and business programs.

Brown University

Located in Providence, Rhode Island, Brown is known for its open curriculum and emphasis on student choice.

Dartmouth College

Located in Hanover, New Hampshire, Dartmouth is known for its strong undergraduate focus and outdoor programs.

Cornell University

Located in Ithaca, New York, Cornell offers a wide range of programs including agriculture and engineering.

University of Pennsylvania

Located in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, UPenn is known for its strong business and medical programs.
