General Resources

Here are some general resources that can help you with your college search and application process:

Student-Made Vocabulary List

A comprehensive list of important vocabulary terms related to college admissions and the application process.

Other Useful Resources:

  • College Board - A comprehensive resource for college planning, including SAT registration, college search, and financial aid information.
  • Common App - A platform that allows you to apply to multiple colleges with one application.
  • Niche - A website that provides reviews and rankings of colleges based on various factors.
  • Princeton Review - A resource for college rankings, test prep, and admissions advice.
  • Appily - A platform that helps you find colleges, scholarships, and provides admissions insights.
  • EC Database - A website that compiles hundreds of profiles of students who have been accepted to top colleges. Highly recommended.
