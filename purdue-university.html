Purdue University

Purdue University is a public research university located in West Lafayette, Indiana. It is known for its strong programs in engineering, agriculture, and science. Founded in 1869, Purdue has grown to become one of the leading research universities in the United States.

Applicant Information

Purdue University isn't a very competitve school, as it is fairly accessible to students from a wide range of backgrounds. Purdue has a self-reported acceptance rate of 43.4%.

Purdue considers the following factors:

  • High school GPA:The middle 50% of admitted students have a GPA between 3.7 and 4.0
  • Standardized Test Scores (SAT/ACT):The middle 50% of admitted students have SAT scores between 1220 and 1480, or ACT scores between 28 and 34
  • Major Choice: Purdue has emphasized that majors like Nursing and Computer Science are more competitve, so your choice of major can impact your chances of admission.
  • "Demonstrated Interest": Purdue does consider wether or not a student connects to Purdue, wether that be by visiting the campus, emailing an admissions officer, or contacting through social media.
  • Letters of Recommendation, Extracurricular Activities, First-Generation Student Status: All factors that are also considered in the admissions process.

Financial Aid and Scholarships

Purdue offers many different scholarships, all of which are extremely competitive. You are automatically considered for merit-based aid when you apply for admission, but you must also complete the FAFSA to be considered for need-based aid. Most scholarhips are based on GPA as well as a holistic review, and range in value from full tuition to $1,000 per year. Purdue's need-based aid is given in the form of loans and work-study programs.

Overall, Purdue is a match school for most and a safety school for a few. It has excellent programs in many fields, and affordability tends to not be an issue for many students.
